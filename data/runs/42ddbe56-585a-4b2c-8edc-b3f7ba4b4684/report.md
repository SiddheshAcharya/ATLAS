## What the field agrees on

The field agrees that AI, particularly deep neural network algorithms, demonstrates significant promise and has been widely applied to medical imaging applications [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.20414, doi:10.18280/isi.270117]. Federated learning is considered well-suited for medical image analysis because it enables collaborative model training across institutions while handling isolated multicenter data and preserving participant privacy [arxiv:2504.05238, doi:10.1002/mp.18064, doi:10.1109/jbhi.2025.3631706]. Early studies applied FedAvg to medical imaging, evaluating its effectiveness on breast density classification using the BI-RADS dataset [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.20414, doi:10.1109/jbhi.2025.3631706]. Furthermore, federated learning frameworks have been shown to improve segmentation performance and model generalization when evaluated on medical imaging datasets [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.20414, doi:10.1109/aiei69164.2026.11497944].
